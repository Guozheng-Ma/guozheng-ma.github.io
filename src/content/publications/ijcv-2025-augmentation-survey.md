This survey organizes data augmentation methods for visual RL around two questions: how to produce augmented data, and how to use it during learning. It connects a unified framework, a taxonomy of methods and empirical evaluations to the challenges of sample efficiency and generalization. [Read the journal publication](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-025-02472-w) or [the arXiv summary](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.04561).

The journal article appeared in 2025; the first preprint was released in 2022.
