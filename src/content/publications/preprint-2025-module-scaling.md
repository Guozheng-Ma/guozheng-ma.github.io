This study compares dynamic training strategies across encoders, critics and actors, and examines their interaction with network architecture. It argues for module-specific training choices rather than applying one sparse-training rule uniformly to every component. The analysis distinguishes topology changes from architectural changes as contributors to improved network scalability.

The linked preprint is also available under its earlier title, *Rethinking the Role of Dynamic Sparse Training for Scalable Deep Reinforcement Learning*.

[Publication record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12096).
