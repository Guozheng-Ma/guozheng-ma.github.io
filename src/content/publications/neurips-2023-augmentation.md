Which properties make augmentation useful for visual RL? This study motivates Random PadResize, which introduces spatial variation with limited distortion, and Cycling Augmentation, which alternates augmentation types over time. Experiments cover DeepMind Control and CARLA. [Read the conference record](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/bc26087d3f82e62044fc77752e86737e-Abstract-Conference.html).
