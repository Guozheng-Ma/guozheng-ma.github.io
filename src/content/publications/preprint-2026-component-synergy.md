This work examines how reinforcement learning components interact and why combining effective components can still hurt performance. ROSER coordinates model-based representation, optimization stability and experience replay using insights from these interactions. The evaluation tests these combined choices across continuous-control benchmarks, emphasizing task-dependent effects and the limitations of naive component stacking.
