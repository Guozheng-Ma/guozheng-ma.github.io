Plasticine provides implementations, evaluation metrics and environments for studying plasticity in deep reinforcement learning. Its benchmark spans different levels of non-stationarity, supporting systematic comparison of methods that preserve an agent’s ability to keep learning. Its modular tools make it possible to examine plasticity across standard and continually changing environments.

First released in April 2025; updated in February 2026.

[Publication record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.17490).
